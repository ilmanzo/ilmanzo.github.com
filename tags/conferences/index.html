Intro On Saturday, October 24th, 2026, I had the pleasure of attending Linux Day Milano 2026. For those unfamiliar with it, Linux Day is the premier nationwide event in Italy dedicated to GNU/Linux, Free Software, and open culture. Promoted annually since 2001 by the Italian Linux Society (ILS) on the fourth Saturday of October, it is held simultaneously across dozens of Italian cities thanks to local Linux User Groups (LUGs), student groups, and passionate volunteers.
